Services

What's included in our basic management fee — comprehensive by design.

Financial management

  • Prepare and submit monthly financial statements to Council
  • Provide the treasurer with bank statements and reconciliation
  • Collect all assessments and special assessments for deposit
  • Keep Council advised of all delinquent accounts
  • Review all invoices and prepare cheques for signing by two Council members
  • Prepare and submit end of year tax returns and regulatory reports
  • Prepare year-end statement of operations and contingency account
  • Prepare the annual budget for council approval prior to the AGM

Administration

  • 24/7 mobile phone service with direct contact to your assigned council assistant
  • Prepare and distribute notices of AGM
  • Attend AGM and council meetings as determined by Council (maximum 90 minutes)
  • Maintain a complete set of financial records on site for two years
  • Upon request, assist Council in selection of employees and contractual services

Property management

  • Establish and activate preventative routine maintenance schedules
  • Monitor the performance of all services provided to the Corporation
  • Monitor maintenance and control of all common areas and equipment
  • Establish and maintain all Corporation contracts, insurance policies and agreements
  • Assist Council with Bylaws, Rules, Regulations and common property issues
  • Upon request, assist Council to obtain a contingency plan and cash flow projection
