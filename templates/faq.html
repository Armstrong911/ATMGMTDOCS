Frequently asked questions

Common questions about strata management and working with Hutton.

Why Hutton?+
We believe condominiums are as varied in their needs as the people living in them — we tailor our contract to your requirements. We provide your strata council with the fullest possible data to enable informed decisions, then make sure those decisions are successfully and expeditiously implemented. We have carefully limited our growth so as not to compromise service quality.
Who will our council assistant be?+
All council assistants are licensed, insured, and trained to handle all aspects of strata property needs. A partner and shareholder of the company will oversee all aspects of your agreement, ensuring a vested interest in your future.
What if our manager is ill or on vacation?+
We ensure that the buildings we manage are always covered by another manager in the event of an absence.
What about after-hours emergencies?+
Hutton managers take emergency calls for their respective buildings. Your assigned strata manager will take appropriate action based on the nature of the emergency.
Who makes the decisions?+
The strata council retains control of the decision-making process at all times.
What about financial statements?+
Financial statements are provided monthly to all council members. You decide how often they are distributed to other owners.
How is our money handled?+
Hutton offers two models: your strata opens its own bank accounts whereby we review invoices and present cheques monthly for two council members to sign — or Hutton oversees your strata's trust accounts with Council approving payables. You remain in control of your money at all times.
What experience do you have?+
As pioneers of condominium management since 1977, our experience is second to none. We have built a portfolio of more than fifty condominium complexes containing more than three thousand homes throughout Greater Victoria and the Capital Regional District.
What will it cost?+
The cost depends on the level of services required and frequency of meetings. Contact us to discuss your strata's needs.
